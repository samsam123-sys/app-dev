# My Favorite Series: *Stranger Things*

> "Friends don't lie." — Eleven

**Stranger Things** is a sci-fi mystery series set in the 1980s town of Hawkins, Indiana, where supernatural events and secret government experiments collide.

## Why I Like It
* **Nostalgic 80s Vibe:** Great music, retro fashion, and classic adventure energy.
* **Strong Characters:** Watching the group grow up and stick together through tough situations.
* **Suspense & Mystery:** Every season brings new twists and lore to uncover.

## Top Characters
1. **Eleven** – Telekinetic abilities and fierce loyalty.
2. **Jim Hopper** – Gruff sheriff with a big heart.
3. **Steve Harrington** – One of the best character arcs in the show.
